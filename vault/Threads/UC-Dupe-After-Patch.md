## Vehicle/Storage Chest Duplication (italokohler, Jul 3, 2025)

1. Place items you want to duplicate inside your vehicle
2. For each item, place one in the storage chest
3. Log out of the game and wait about 30 seconds
4. Log back in
5. Open the vehicle's storage — it should be empty
6. Use E button to activate "Take All" function to remove everything
7. Press F on the storage chest to use "Quick Deposit" feature
8. Store the vehicle as normal
9. Restore the vehicle — items now in BOTH the chest and the vehicle
