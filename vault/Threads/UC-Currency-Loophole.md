## Bank NPC Solaris Duplication (a1771519148, Jul 14, 2025)

**Status: Disputed — patched per some users, OP says still working Jul 17**

### Simple Method (OP)
1. Go to bank NPC, withdraw all your money
2. Immediately respawn (die)
3. After respawn, return to death location and pick up your backpack — regain original money
4. Open bank NPC — withdrawn money has returned and been doubled

### Detailed Steps (a1771519148, post #4, credited to EroKuni)

**Setup:** Start with 0 in bank, test with small amount first (e.g., 200k Solaris)

**Step 1:**
1. Talk to Bank NPC
2. Deposit 200k Solaris, then withdraw it right away
3. Respawn (die)
4. Collect money, deposit back into bank

**Step 2:**
1. Respawn again but do NOT withdraw Solaris
2. Talk to Bank NPC — deposit/withdraw options missing
3. **Do NOT open inventory** (breaks the glitch)

**Step 3:**
1. Leave Harko/Arak and come back
2. Bank now displays original Solaris amount (200k)

**Step 4:**
1. Withdraw money and respawn again
2. Repeat — amount keeps increasing

### Limitations
- **Cap:** 1,750,000 Solaris (max inventory can hold)
- **Resets:** After leaving Harko/Arak, the glitch stops working
- **Reset fix:** Delete dummy character, glitch works again
- **Critical:** Do NOT open inventory during glitch — it resets the amount to what you actually have
